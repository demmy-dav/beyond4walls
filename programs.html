Metamorphosis

A masterclass on transformation. Nobody arrives fully formed: every creator, founder, builder and business owner grows through stages, each one changing what they can make and who they become.

Past Program · 2025

The Idea Nexus Conference 2025

  • Students
  • Founders
  • Builders
  • Visionaries

The Idea Nexus Conference 2025, themed Ideation to Execution: Transforming Ideas into National Growth, brought students together at Lead City University, Ibadan, as a launchpad for visionaries.

A day of keynotes, fireside chats and panels on turning ideas into real-world results, ending with prize and scholarship awards for the standout ideas in the room.

A group of young change-makers bridging the gap between the classroom and the real world.
